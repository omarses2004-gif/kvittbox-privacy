Kvittbox Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Kvittbox – Receipt & Warranty Keeper keeps your receipts on your phone and reminds you before a return window or warranty ends. This page explains what happens with your data. The short version: it stays on your phone.

What Kvittbox stores, and where

  • Your receipts (photos and PDFs), the details you enter or the app reads from them (store, date, total, notes), return and warranty dates, and your settings.
  • All of this is stored only in Kvittbox's private storage on your phone. Other apps can't read it.
  • Kvittbox has no account, no server, no ads, no analytics and no trackers. We, the makers of Kvittbox, never receive your data.
  • Android's own automatic app backup is turned off for Kvittbox, so your receipts are not copied to the cloud without you starting it.

Reading receipts

Scanning and reading the text on a receipt happen on your phone, using Google's ML Kit and the document scanner in Google Play services. Your receipt images and their text are not sent anywhere. Kvittbox turns off ML Kit's own usage statistics. The scanner screen itself is part of Google Play services and is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Reminders

Reminders are notifications scheduled on your phone. They don't use the internet.

Backup to your Google Drive (optional)

  • Only when you start it, Kvittbox saves a backup in a hidden app folder of your own Google Drive.
  • The backup is encrypted on your phone with your backup password before it leaves the phone. Google can't read it, and neither can we.
  • To reach your Drive, you sign in with your Google account. Kvittbox only gets access to its own hidden folder, not to your other files. Signing in is handled by Google and covered by Google's privacy policy.

Unlocking the app

The one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited receipts goes through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles the payment; Kvittbox only learns whether the purchase was made. Your receipts are never involved.

App lock

If you turn on the app lock, Kvittbox asks your phone to confirm it's you with your fingerprint, face, PIN, pattern or password. Your phone does the check; Kvittbox never sees your fingerprint, face or PIN.

Sharing and exporting

When you share a receipt, download files or export a CSV list, the files go where you choose (for example an email or a folder). These files are not encrypted. What happens to them then is up to you and the app you share with.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show reminders.
  • Run at startup: to schedule reminders again after the phone restarts.
  • Internet: only for the optional Drive backup and the Google Play purchase.
  • Fingerprint / face: only if you turn on the app lock.

Kvittbox does not ask for camera or storage permission: the scanner and the system file pickers only give it the files you choose.

Deleting your data

Delete a receipt in the app, or uninstall Kvittbox to remove everything on the phone. To remove a Drive backup, remove Kvittbox's access in your Google account settings ("Third-party apps & services"), which also deletes its hidden folder.

Children

Kvittbox is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
